# Paper figures and tables

Generated by `python make_paper.py` from the stored results (do not edit by
hand; re-run the script). Figures are PDF (a PNG preview sits next to each).
Tables are bare `tabular` environments; they need `\usepackage{booktabs,amssymb}`
and are meant to be wrapped in your own `table` environment with a caption.
`preview.tex` (also generated) inputs everything, including the
acknowledgments (`acknowledgments.tex`, written by hand); compile it on
Overleaf to check the layout. Clients appear in the same order everywhere:
by training share, largest first (Heart: Cleveland, Hungary, VA,
Switzerland; TCGA: Northeast, West, South, Midwest, Europe, Canada; ISIC:
centres 0-5). `make_paper.py` checks automatically that every relative
influence printed in `fig_who_pays` equals the cell of the influence tables
for the same rule and client (it stops with an error otherwise).

Conventions (all items): points = metric × 100; mean ± 95% t CI over seeds;
"(w)" = weighted by training size; Krum ignores weights, so it has one row.
"Mean of real attacks" = per seed, the mean over every non-degenerate
attack and every attacker identity (f = 1: each client; f = 2: every pair).
The server assumes f_assumed = f in clean runs. Claims supported:
1. validity must be judged by data share;
2. a big client's "attack" is mostly lost data for count-based rules and
   real damage for weight-dependent rules;
3. weighting gives the largest client power in proportion to its share;
4. weighting helps while the big client is honest;
5. who pays follows update distance, not size;
6. averages hide damage to single clients;
7. known attack patterns reproduce;
8. staying out costs a hospital on its own test set.

## Notes for captions

- TCGA's worst region counts only regions with ≥ 3 test deaths (Northeast,
  South, West, Midwest); its test split is easier than its training data,
  so compare rules, not absolute C-indices.
- ISIC worst centre: per-centre balanced accuracy over classes with ≥ 5
  test images (k = 10 in the appendix CSVs).
- Heart at n = 4, f = 1: Krum, Multi-Krum and Bulyan are invalid; the
  unweighted trimmed mean equals the median. Baruch's ALIE has z = 0 at
  n = 4 or 6 with f = 1 and is excluded from real attacks (it is kept at
  f = 2, z = 0.43); `alie_z1` (z = 1) is included everywhere.
- Leave-out decomposition: attacked − clean = (control − clean: data loss)
  + (attacked − control: attack damage); the control trains without the
  attacker(s), same seeds and batch order (exact twins except under the
  Gaussian attack).
